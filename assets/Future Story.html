Step 1: Paint the Picture of the Current Reality

Begin by describing the present situation in a detailed and relatable way. This establishes the context, highlighting the existing challenges or limitations that are felt by the audience.

Step 2: Introduce the First Turning Point

Introduce a pivotal moment or event that signals the need for change. This turning point emphasizes the urgency and importance of addressing the current issues, inspiring the desire to move toward a better future.

Step 3: State What Could Be (Opportunity)

Present the first vision of a better future, focusing on the potential opportunities. This future state should be inspiring, showing how things can improve dramatically from the current situation.

Step 4: Outline What Is (Problem - an Aspect of Step 1)

Return to an aspect of the current reality to emphasize the problem that still exists. This creates a contrast with the future opportunity, highlighting what needs to be overcome.

Step 5: State Another Example of What Could Be (Opportunity)

Present another vision of the future, showing further opportunities for positive change. This reinforces the idea that transformation is possible and desirable.

Step 6: Outline What Is (Problem - Another Aspect of Step 1)

Highlight another challenge or limitation in the current reality. This builds tension and motivates the audience to recognize the need for change.

Step 7: State Another Example of What Could Be (Opportunity)

Offer a third vision of the future, showcasing more possibilities that align with the desired transformation. This further strengthens the contrast between the present and the future.

Step 8: Outline What Is (Problem - Another Aspect of Step 1)

Bring attention to yet another challenge from the current reality. By now, the contrast between what is and what could be should be very clear and compelling.

Step 9: Introduce the Second Turning Point

Present a second critical moment that calls for immediate action. This turning point emphasizes the steps needed to transition from the present reality to the future vision and sets the stage for the final push toward change.

Step 10: End on a Higher Plane

Conclude with an uplifting message that reassures the audience of the value and importance of the journey ahead. Highlight the potential for success and positive outcomes, encouraging them to commit to the necessary actions to achieve the future vision.
