Finding Your Ikigai

Reflect and identify activities that matter to you. Enter items in the text box below, and for each item, select one or more of the four pillars of Ikigai that apply to the activity. This exercise requires a thoughtful approach to each area of your personal or professional life, framed by what you’re good at, what you love, what provides value to you, and what provides value to others.

Core Pillars

These are the foundational elements of Ikigai — What You Love, What You Are Good At, What You Can Be Paid For, and What the World Needs. Reflect on each pillar to identify activities and values that resonate with you.
